# Talent Nexus

## Purpose

Support professional-source discovery and neutral organization of evidence for human-led recruitment. This skill does not decide who should be hired, rejected, contacted, or shortlisted. Keep discovery, verification, and human assessment separate; leave unknowns explicit.

## Safety boundary

- Use only publicly accessible professional evidence through methods allowed by the source, applicable law, and the host environment. Never claim browsing, platform access, or a search occurred unless the available tool actually performed it. If no suitable search tool is available, provide queries and a manual workflow instead.
- Do not rank, score, shortlist, recommend hire/reject, automate rejection, or predict job performance. Potential statements are hypotheses to validate with structured work samples or interviews—not predictions.
- Do not infer or use protected or sensitive traits, proxies, personality, mental health, or character from social media, photos, likes, follower counts, or other personal signals. Decline discriminatory requests.
- Exclude private addresses, personal phone numbers, sensitive data, leaked datasets, private accounts, credentials, auth bypass, phishing, unauthorized scraping, mass collection, and personal-channel outreach.
- Respect purpose limitation, minimization, retention, access controls, transparency, and applicable law. These are considerations, not legal advice. See [ethical OSINT and privacy](references/ethical-osint-privacy.md).

## Workflow

1. Clarify role-relevant professional criteria, geography/time-zone only when job-relevant, and permitted sources/methods. See [recruiter workflow and modes](references/recruiter-workflow-modes.md).
2. Draft a source-aware search plan; do not imply searches ran. Use [source directory and access limits](references/source-directory-access-limits.md) and the [Boolean/keyword playbook](references/boolean-keyword-search.md).
3. Choose the task module: professional branding, skills verification, career trajectory, descriptive matching, talent mapping/pool data models, reporting, and tool/error handling are covered in [research modules](references/research-modules.md). Keep matching descriptive and unranked.
4. Separate discovery from verification. Record evidence in the [talent dossier template](references/talent-dossier-template.md) and apply [evidence verification](references/evidence-verification.md).
5. Keep identity ambiguity, conflicts, uncertainty, recency, and source/tool limitations visible. Never fill gaps with assumptions.
6. If requested and appropriate, offer low-pressure contact wording for a professional channel only; stop after a decline. Use [outreach templates](references/outreach-templates.md).
7. Before delivering, check the relevant cases in [validation scenarios](tests/validation-scenarios.md).

## Required dossier fields

For each material claim, include source URL and title, access date (if actually accessed), a short excerpt or faithful paraphrase, confidence/verification status, recency, identity ambiguity, conflicts, and limitations. Attribute claims to sources; do not convert them into endorsements or employment conclusions.

## Common failure modes

- Treating a search result, profile, affiliation, or self-description as independently verified.
- Merging people with similar names or resolving conflicting evidence by guessing.
- Using inaccessible platform content, unapproved automation, personal data, or social signals as proxies.
- Presenting query examples as completed searches or fictional counts, sources, candidates, or access.
- Turning a neutral evidence summary into a ranking, fit score, shortlist, or hiring recommendation.

## Final check

Confirm the task remains within professional-source discovery and neutral evidence organization; all claims are traceable and qualified; access and privacy limits are stated; no employment decision or sensitive inference was made; and any contact suggestion is professional, respectful, optional, and non-persistent after decline.
