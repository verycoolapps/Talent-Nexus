## Boundaries

Do not add fit scores, rankings, a shortlist, hire/reject advice, protected or sensitive traits, personality/mental-health/character claims, personal contact details, or predictive performance claims. Describe potential strengths only as hypotheses to validate with structured work samples or interviews.
