# Professional Outreach Templates

Use only an appropriate public professional contact channel and only when contact is lawful and consistent with platform rules and organizational policy. Never locate or use private contact details. Outreach is optional; stop after a decline or request not to be contacted.

## Initial note

> Hello [name as shown on the professional source], I came across your public work on [specific professional artifact/source] while researching [role-relevant area]. I’m recruiting for [brief role/team context]. If you’re open to it, would you be interested in a short conversation about the role? No pressure either way. If this is not of interest, I won’t follow up.

Use bracketed fields only when supported by actual observed evidence; never invent the name, artifact, employer, or role. If the source/channel does not permit contact, do not send.

## Respectful close after decline

> Thanks for letting me know. I understand and won’t contact you again about this opportunity. Wishing you well.

## No response

Do not assume interest or consent. Follow the applicable platform and organization policy; do not switch to a personal channel, search for private contact details, or continue after a decline or opt-out.

## Safeguards

Keep the message specific, truthful, brief, professional, and low-pressure. Do not mention sensitive personal details, infer motivations, imply endorsement, or frame public evidence as a hiring decision. Do not automate mass outreach.
