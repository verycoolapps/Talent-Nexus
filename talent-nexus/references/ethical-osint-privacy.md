# Ethical OSINT and Privacy

Professional-source discovery can affect employment opportunity and privacy. Keep the purpose narrow: identify and organize relevant public professional evidence for human review—not infer personal worth or make a decision.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No protected or sensitive-trait inference or proxy discrimination, including from names, photos, location, affiliations, social activity, or other indirect signals.
- No personality, mental-health, character, or performance prediction from social media, images, likes, follower counts, or personal behavior.
- Do not collect or expose private addresses, personal phone numbers, sensitive data, leaked datasets, private-account content, credentials, or personal-channel contact details.
- No credential use, auth bypass, phishing, unauthorized scraping, mass collection, or circumvention of platform restrictions. Public visibility does not grant blanket permission for automation or reuse.
- Decline discriminatory or unauthorized personal-data requests; briefly explain the boundary and offer a role-relevant, privacy-preserving alternative.

## Privacy-by-design checklist

- **Purpose limitation:** define role-relevant purpose and allowed sources before discovery.
- **Minimization:** collect only evidence needed for that purpose; avoid sensitive and unnecessary identifiers.
- **Retention:** keep information only as long as the legitimate stated purpose requires; define deletion/retention controls with the responsible organization.
- **Access controls:** restrict dossier access to authorized reviewers; avoid unnecessary copying or distribution.
- **Transparency:** identify the source and method, distinguish observed from unverified, and be candid about limitations.
- **Applicable rules:** consider Indonesia’s UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi, applicable employment law, platform terms, organizational policy, and other context-relevant requirements. Application depends on context; this is not legal advice. Consult qualified counsel or a privacy officer when needed.

Never treat absence of public evidence as evidence of lack of ability or interest. Candidate contact is optional, professional-channel-only, low-pressure, and must stop after a decline.

## Human-reviewed transparency and correction

Where appropriate, surface the sources and methods used. Route disputed or potentially inaccurate information to the responsible human or data owner, and allow the candidate to clarify it before reliance. Keep unresolved claims explicit and do not use them for automated adverse action.
